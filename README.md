# ubuhleonline

Create a sleek, professional, and feminine landing page for Ubuhle — a South African tech-beauty platform that connects clients with trusted hair and beauty professionals through smart technology.

The site should feel modern, empowering, and tech-forward, while still warm and relatable to everyday beauty users.

🏠 Page Layout

1. Header / Navigation

Top left: Ubuhle logo.

Right side: navigation links — Style Previewing, Service Providers, Find Stylist, and a purple Get Started button.

Sticky header on scroll.

2. Hero Section

Large image of a confident Black woman with natural hair, facing right.

Headline: “Connecting you to the right people, faster.”

Subtext: “Using your style preview to instantly match you with trusted providers tailored to your needs.”

CTA button: Get Started.

Floating callouts with text like: “Style Preview Generated,” “Secure Booking in Progress,” and “Best Provider for You.”

Background uses soft circular tech motifs.

3. Mission Section

Background: soft lavender with faint geometric patterns.

Title: “Our mission is to connect beauty professionals with clients through seamless, smart technology.”

Supporting paragraph describing how Ubuhle bridges the gap between clients and businesses.

4. Service Offerings Section

Section title: “Everything you need to discover, book and manage beauty services.”

Four cards in a grid layout:

Always-On: 24/7 booking and management.

Online Bookings, Secure Payments: Real-time scheduling and safe payments.

Stylist Matching and Discovery: AI-generated previews using selfies.

Business Tools: Scheduling, analytics, and portfolio management for providers.

Each card has a minimalist line icon and short text.

5. Our Technology Section

Title: “Fast. Easy. Reliable.”

Text: Explains that users upload a selfie and style inspiration photo to see an AI-generated look before booking.

Visual carousel: horizontally sliding images of clients → inspiration → generated previews (looping automatically).

CTA: Try It Out Now.

6. Footer Section

Left: navigation links (Style Previewing, Service Providers, Stylist Search, Company, Terms & Conditions).

Center: tagline repeated — “Connecting you to the right people, faster.”

Bottom: oversized, transparent “UBUHLE” text watermark across the footer background.

🎨 Style & Motion

Minimalist and polished, with soft drop shadows and rounded edges.

Floating UI elements fade in and subtly move upward as the page loads.

Carousel slides automatically every 3–4 seconds with smooth easing.

CTA buttons use hover effects that gently glow in lavender.

The page should reflect the fusion of beauty and technology — refined, empowering, and unmistakably African in tone but global in quality.
